01 · Academic planning

GWA Target Planner

A planner for students aiming at Latin honors under the PUP grade scale. Enter your completed semesters, pick a target, and it works out the grades you still need, weighted by units and recalculated as you type.

  • Summa, Magna, and Cum Laude targets
  • Flags grades that would disqualify honors
  • Printable plan for advising sessions

02 · Monetary economics

Dequiña Rounds Requirement

In money and banking, it is hard to guess how many lending rounds it takes before deposit creation reaches 99% of the multiplier's limit. This calculator answers that, alongside the simple money multiplier, maximum deposits, and maximum loans.

n = ⌈ log(1 − t) / log(1 − r) ⌉
