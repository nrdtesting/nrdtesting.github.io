Section 01

About the Study

Infrastructure plays a vital role in supporting economic growth and development by allowing the efficient movement of goods, services, and people.

As investments in infrastructure facilitate the expansion of industries, improve access to markets, and support the functioning of economic activities, they have been widely recognized as a key driver of productivity (Srinivasa & Rao, 2013). Specifically, transportation infrastructure functions as an essential component of this system, as it allows regions to connect, trade, and develop more effectively.

Among different transport infrastructure types, roads specifically, play a crucial role in connecting rural areas to urban economic centers, improving access and encouraging economic participation (Fruelda et al., 2025). Infrastructure development also drives economic growth through a cumulative process that increases investment, output, income, and employment, while also reducing transaction costs and improving competitiveness (Srinivasa & Rao, 2013).

Key Insight

Through these roles, road networks not only support production and trade but also contribute to wider economic outcomes such as employment generation and regional development.
