Profile

I am a BS Economics student who enjoys the slow part of research: gathering the data, cleaning it, and checking whether the story still holds once the numbers are in.

Most of my academic work deals with public infrastructure, regional employment, and economic policy. I lead groups often, mostly because someone has to keep the timeline honest, and I have found that I like that role. Outside class, I build small web tools that remove repetitive computation from student life.

Alongside my studies, I worked full-time in food service for almost a year. Balancing a closing shift with a problem set is a good lesson in managing scarce resources, which is, after all, the first thing economics teaches.
